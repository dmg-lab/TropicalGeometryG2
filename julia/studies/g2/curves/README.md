# The curves of genus ten (Section 10)

The Mukai model of genus 10 is G2/P2 in P^13. Method 1 intersects trop(G2/P2) with a translate q + B_4 of the Bergman
fan B_4 of the uniform matroid of rank 17 on 21 elements; Method 2 with five random shifted tropical hyperplanes. The
runs that gave the two curves of the paper are in [`curves_g10/overnight_20261028/`](../../../curves_g10/overnight_20261028/);
the curves are `data/mukai_curves/genus_10_method_{1,2}.mrdi`, written by the stage `zenodo` of the census. Their
figures are drawn by `to_tex/curve_portraits.jl` and `to_tex/build_curve_atlas.jl`. Run everything from `julia/` with
`julia --project=.`.

## The Mukai model

- [`mukai_models.jl`](mukai_models.jl): the model by the 4x4 Pfaffians of a 7x7 matrix and a random linear section in
  P^8, the 18 points of (d) (dimension 0, degree 18).
- [`mukai_g10_degenerations.jl`](mukai_g10_degenerations.jl): in `genus10_setup`, the ideal is generated by 28 quadrics,
  of dimension 5 and degree 18, as in the Betti table; the rest explores one-parameter degenerations of the model
  (about an hour).

## Method 1

- [`uniform_bergman_fan.jl`](uniform_bergman_fan.jl): B_4, the input `data/fan_uniform_linear_space_16_21.mrdi`
  (an existing file is only compared).
- [`run_experiments.jl`](run_experiments.jl) (and [`run_experiments.slurm`](run_experiments.slurm) for hydra) with
  [`intersection_lib.jl`](intersection_lib.jl): random translated intersections.
- [`method_1_curve.jl`](method_1_curve.jl): the curve of Method 1 exactly from its shift q; the stage `zenodo` calls it,
  and [`curve_complex.jl`](curve_complex.jl) makes the two curves Oscar complexes.
- [`bergman_curve.jl`](bergman_curve.jl): the curve C ∩ (B + t), exactly, cell by cell, with the multiplicities of its
  pieces; the library of `method_1_curve.jl`, `curve_rays.jl`, `core_trivalence.jl` and `bounded_cell_valences.jl`.
- [`intersect_complexes.jl`](intersect_complexes.jl): the feasibility filters of `intersection_lib.jl` in their own
  module.
- [`shift_basis.jl`](shift_basis.jl): the basis `data/mukai_curves/genus_10_shift_basis.mrdi` of the translations.

```bash
julia --project=. studies/g2/curves/run_experiments.jl GENUS [COUNT] [SOLVER] [OUTPUT_DIR]
```

`GENUS` is 10. `SOLVER` is `highs`, `soplex`, `hybrid`, or `certified`; the default is `hybrid`. `certified` is
`hybrid` without its SoPlex fallback: the pairs HiGHS cannot certify are kept, and the exact intersections discard the
infeasible ones, so it is exact too. Use it where SoPlex crashes.
Without `OUTPUT_DIR`, checkpoints are written beneath the ignored `results/curves/genus_GENUS/`. Existing
complete checkpoints are resumed; a folder with the `.jls` checkpoints of earlier versions is refused. Each experiment
stores its translation, graph, intersected polyhedra, and result record as Oscar `.mrdi` files; `all_results.mrdi`
summarizes every completed experiment.

By default a translation is uniformly random. With `--shift-basis`, it is a random integral combination of the rows of
a basis, with coefficients drawn uniformly from `--coefficients=LOW:HIGH`, by default `-32768:32768`, stored beside the
translation as `coefficients_K.mrdi`. The default basis is `data/mukai_curves/genus_10_shift_basis.mrdi`; pass
`--shift-basis=FILE` for another.

```bash
julia --project=. studies/g2/curves/run_experiments.jl 10 5 certified results/curves/genus_10/shift_basis --shift-basis
```

## Method 2

- [`hyperplane_intersection_census.jl`](hyperplane_intersection_census.jl) with
  [`tropical_hyperplanes.jl`](tropical_hyperplanes.jl): the curve sections by 5 random shifted tropical hyperplanes.
- [`sample_genus_10.jl`](sample_genus_10.jl) (with [`sample_genus_10.slurm`](sample_genus_10.slurm) and
  [`sample_genus_10_report.sh`](sample_genus_10_report.sh)): fast sampling for a curve of genus 10 with trivalent core.
- [`point_filter.jl`](point_filter.jl): the 18 points of a section, a filter in front of
  [`core_trivalence.jl`](core_trivalence.jl), which tests whether the core is trivalent.
- [`curve_details.jl`](curve_details.jl): the core and the points of a curve of the census.

## The curves and the K3 surface

- [`decorated_curve_graph.jl`](decorated_curve_graph.jl): the curve as a graph decorated by the cells it crosses, and
  the helpers of every curve script (multiplicities of the cells, balancing and degree).
- [`curve_rays.jl`](curve_rays.jl): the rays with their multiplicities, and the degree 18 in every coordinate.
- [`shifted_bergman_cells.jl`](shifted_bergman_cells.jl), [`bounded_cell_valences.jl`](bounded_cell_valences.jl): the
  cells whose part of the curve is a polytope, and the valences there.
- [`k3_surface_polygons.jl`](k3_surface_polygons.jl), [`k3_fins.jl`](k3_fins.jl),
  [`k3_core_regions.jl`](k3_core_regions.jl): the tropical K3 surface X18 that contains the curve of Method 1 (written
  by the stage zenodo to `data/mukai_curves/genus_10_method_1_k3.mrdi`), its fins, which collapse away onto a 2-sphere,
  and its match with the region atlas.
