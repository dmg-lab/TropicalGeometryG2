# Characteristic p: certificates for the tropical G2 flag variety

Proof of Theorem `thm:flagchar2` of `main.tex` for odd p: trop_p(F) = trop(F).  Same method as
for the Grassmannian (`../README.md`).  MIN convention; J over ZZ is generated by the 78
generators of `julia/data/g2/flag/00_ideal.mrdi` (coefficients +-1).  Run from `julia/` with
`julia --project=. --heap-size-hint=2500M studies/g2/char-p/flag/SCRIPT.jl`, one process at a
time (about 2.6 GB; `verify.jl` was OOM-killed at 4 GB without the heap hint).

## First inclusion: `basis.jl` -> `results/basis.txt`, `certificates/basis.mrdi` (1 min)

- The 12 taxon permutations act by signed permutations x_i -> s_i x_sigma(i),
  p_ij -> s_i s_j p_sigma(i)sigma(j) that preserve J over ZZ.
- The three cubics of `eq:threecubics` lie in J over ZZ (integer cofactors).
- The generators have coefficients +-1, so trop_p(F) lies in the flag Dressian D
  (`julia/data/g2/flag_dressian/02_coarsest_fan.mrdi`) for every p.  The cells of D outside
  trop(F) are the 33 4-simplices and 15 of their facets; every cell of dimension <= 7 lies in
  trop(F).  On the relative interior of each of these 48 cells, the 2-term of one of the three
  cubics is the initial form (5 facets and 11 simplices per cubic).
- Hence the 78 generators and the three cubics form a tropical basis of J in every
  characteristic other than 2.

## Second inclusion: `compute.jl` -> `certificates/`, `verify.jl` -> `results/verify.csv`

One certificate per D12-orbit of maximal cones of `julia/data/g2/flag/01_raw_fan.mrdi` (270
orbits; `cones/cone_NNNN.mrdi`, NNNN = smallest index in the orbit).  A cone C is covered by
pieces P, each with the reduced Groebner basis G of J over ZZ for the weight order of
w = sum of the rays of P (refined by degrevlex) and a torus point (+-1 point found by an
exhaustive search, else a slice certificate as in `../compute.jl`).  P = C when the initial
forms are constant on relint C; otherwise the pieces are the Groebner cones meeting the
interior of C, found by stepping across facets until their volumes add up.  Membership of
each G in J: division by one Groebner basis G0 (`base.mrdi`) whose integer cofactors are stored.

`verify.jl` re-checks all of it without Groebner computations: the pieces cover C (contained in
C, disjoint interiors, volumes add up), G in J, generators in <G>, S-polynomials with standard
representations over ZZ, leading coefficients powers of 2, homogeneity for the lineality,
the ray conditions on P, the torus point; and the representatives and their images under the
12 coordinate permutations are all 2714 cones.

Result (2026-11-14): all checks pass for all 389 pieces.  226 orbits need one piece (C itself),
44 are subdivided (2-19 Groebner cones).  Leading coefficients of G: 1, except {1, 2} in orbits
381, 416, 426, 695, 1044 and {1, 2, 4} in orbit 1768.  Torus points: 353 pieces by +-1 points,
36 pieces (20 orbits) by slices with leading coefficients powers of 2 (up to 2^26).
416755 S-polynomials checked, none failed.  `compute.jl` takes about 5 min; `verify.jl` about
15 min and needs restarts (`--resume`): its memory grows to the 4 GB cap after about 190 orbits.
Certificates: 20 MB.
